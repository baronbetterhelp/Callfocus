# CallFocus V14.3 — Full-Screen Payment Confirmation

- Manual bank-transfer approval now triggers a full-screen success experience instead of remaining inside the payment modal card.
- The green confirmation check animates at large scale in the center of the screen.
- Success copy confirms that the credits were added and tells the customer they are being taken back to the Credits page.
- The success screen remains visible for about 4.2 seconds before redirecting automatically.
- Payment request, receipt upload, admin/email approval, rejection, wallet crediting, and pricing logic are unchanged.
