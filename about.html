About CallFocus at callfocus.link

CallFocus is a web-based conversation continuity platform for realtime voice calls. It is designed to keep caller background, conversation dynamics and what is new today connected across repeat calls.

Official websitehttps://callfocus.link
What CallFocus does

Prepare once, continue with context.

CallFocus lets users prepare information about themselves and the person they are calling, organize conversation dynamics, add what today’s call is about, attach locations and time zones, select a voice, and keep that setup connected to recent-call threads for later conversations.

  • Realtime voice calls with reusable context
  • Saved callers and repeat-call continuity
  • Conversation dynamics generation
  • Voice notes and saved information
  • Worldwide location and time-zone awareness
  • Live translation transcript when needed
Name clarification

A separate product from Call Focus in Ireland.

CallFocus at callfocus.link is an independent web software product. It is not affiliated with Call Focus at callfocus.ie, the Irish business training and consultancy company associated with Karen Sommerville.

This page exists to make the identity of the CallFocus product and its official website clear to users, search engines and automated systems.
