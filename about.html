What CallFocus does

Prepare once, continue with context.

CallFocus combines calling, voice, video creation and reusable conversation context. Users can prepare information about themselves and the person they are calling, organize conversation dynamics, select a voice and return to previous call threads. Separate tools provide permanent voice cloning, AI voice notes, Live AI Avatar and Image to Video.

  • Real-time OpenAI voice calls with caller context and controls
  • Permanent ElevenLabs voice cloning from your own consented recording (limited slots)
  • Custom AI voice profiles and AI voice notes
  • Live AI Avatar and Image to Video
  • Saved callers, screenshot-assisted context and repeat-call threads
  • Conversation dynamics generation and natural call planning
  • Worldwide location, time-zone awareness and live translation
  • Account credits, transaction history and referrals
Name clarification

A separate product from Call Focus in Ireland.

CallFocus at callfocus.link is an independent web software product. It is not affiliated with Call Focus at callfocus.ie, the Irish business training and consultancy company associated with Karen Sommerville.

This page exists to make the identity of the CallFocus product and its official website clear to users, search engines and automated systems.

Frequently asked

CallFocus quick facts

What is CallFocus?

A web platform for real-time AI voice calls, permanent voice cloning, voice notes, Live AI Avatar, Image to Video and context-aware conversations.

Can I create a cloned voice?

Yes. Eligible adults can reserve a limited slot, record or upload their own voice with consent, and reuse it for supported cloned calls or voice notes.

Does CallFocus make videos from images?

Yes. Its Image to Video tool can generate short videos from an uploaded image and a motion description when enabled.

Where is the official CallFocus website?

The official website for this product is callfocus.link.

Is CallFocus a business training company?

No. The CallFocus product at callfocus.link is software for conversation continuity and realtime calls.

Is CallFocus affiliated with callfocus.ie?

No. The two are separate and unaffiliated.
