Payment bonuses

Credit packages & extra-credit promotion

Edit how many base credits each fixed NGN package gives. A bonus adds extra credits after a confirmed payment and never reduces the amount charged. New settings apply only to new payment requests.

The minimum amount is validated at checkout. Packages below your chosen minimum will not be available for new checkouts.

Example: ₦10,000 gives 1,000 credits before any promotional bonus.

Customer announcements

Announcement popup

Write and save an announcement independently of credit bonuses. It appears only to signed-in customers, after sign-in and on every refresh while enabled.

Usage pricing

Credits per service

Enter credits for one minute of service. Shorter usage is charged proportionally by seconds. Changes to active calls use their original rate; new sessions use saved rates.

Examples: Voice calls 10 seconds = 16.7 credits; Image to Video 10 seconds = 300 credits.

Image-to-Video rates replace its separate 10/15-second costs when saved here. Other features without usage-based wallet billing are not affected.
