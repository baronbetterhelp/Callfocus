Payment methods

Payment availability

Choose which payment methods appear on CallFocus. Disabled methods disappear from the credit purchase page and cannot start new payment requests.

Each switch controls visibility and new checkout requests. Previously received payment confirmations and pending transfers can still be processed. Paystack is not changed.

When enabled, customers can request your bank account if Paystack is unavailable. Receipt emails are sent through your existing Resend configuration. The uploaded receipt is also available privately from this admin panel for 60 days.
