# CallFocus V14.2 — Detailed Conversation Dynamics

- Expanded the conversation-dynamics generator from a short 120–220 word summary to a detailed, structured profile targeting roughly 550–850 words when the screenshots contain enough information.
- Final output now begins with `Dynamics of Your Conversations`, uses natural paragraph breaks, and can include a concise `*` bullet list for recurring daily topics/routines.
- The generator now separately captures the other person’s communication style, the user’s style, how those styles interact, affection/humor, emotional support, boundaries/tension, recurring life context, relationship progression, unresolved issues, and practical guidance for future CallFocus calls.
- Batch analysis now preserves more concrete detail before finalization instead of over-compressing each screenshot group.
- Names are used only when supported by the conversation; otherwise the result says `the other person`. Unsupported facts and hidden motives must not be invented.
- The generated-result textarea is taller and better suited to longer formatted dynamics.
- Screenshot upload, privacy behavior, editing, copy, and Use in New Call remain unchanged.
