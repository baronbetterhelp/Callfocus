How CallFocus works

Set it once. Continue naturally.

CallFocus separates the permanent caller context from what is new today, so repeat calls do not feel like starting from zero.

01

Prepare the first call

Add the call title, person’s name, both callers’ information, conversation dynamics, today’s topic, both locations/time zones, and the voice.

02

Call with the right context

The live voice session receives the saved relationship context and the details that matter for that specific call.

03

Next time, add only what changed

Open Recent Calls, enter what today’s new conversation is about, and call again. Edit the saved caller information or dynamics only when you want to.

Built around continuity

Your recent calls become reusable conversation threads.

Instead of rebuilding a caller every time, each thread keeps the person, relationship context, conversation dynamics, last-used call location and voice ready for the next call.

Designed for real calls

Useful controls without a cluttered interface.

01

Caller memory

Keep both callers’ background and relationship dynamics attached to the person instead of rewriting them every time.

02

Per-call time awareness

Locations and time zones stay with each call thread, so they can be reused or changed when someone travels.

03

Graceful call ending

Request End asks the voice assistant to wrap up naturally in the tone of that day’s conversation before the call closes.

04

Separate admin controls

Your global voice choices and master call rules live at the private /admin area, away from customer navigation.
