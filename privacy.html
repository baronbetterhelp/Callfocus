Privacy Policy

How CallFocus handles account data, voice features, payments and conversation screenshots.

What CallFocus processes

CallFocus stores account information, your saved caller context, recent-call threads, profile settings and credit information so your workspace can follow you across devices.

Voice calls and voice notes

When you start an AI call or generate a voice note, the information needed for that request is sent to OpenAI to provide the realtime or generated-audio service. CallFocus does not place secret API keys in your browser.

Conversation screenshots

If you choose Generate conversation dynamics, the screenshots you select are prepared in your browser and sent through the CallFocus server to OpenAI for analysis. CallFocus does not save those screenshots to your account or to CallFocus KV storage. The OpenAI request is sent with storage disabled by CallFocus. OpenAI may still process or retain API data according to its own API data-use and security policies.

Payments and email

Payment transactions are handled by Paystack. Transaction references and verified credit records may be stored by CallFocus to prevent duplicate crediting. Transactional account emails are delivered through Resend.

Your choices

  • Only upload conversation content you are allowed to process.
  • You can delete your CallFocus account from Account Settings.
  • You can contact support about privacy or account questions at support@callfocus.link.

Security

CallFocus uses server-side API keys, authenticated account sessions, rate limiting on sensitive account endpoints and private server-side storage for account data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
