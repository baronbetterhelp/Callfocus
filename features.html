Two separate voice options

Voice customization is not the same as voice cloning.

CallFocus offers supported synthetic AI voice profiles for regular calls and a separate permanent voice-cloning feature. The latter uses your own consented voice sample to create one reusable saved voice per eligible customer. Permanent cloning depends on limited slots and provider storage availability.

How access works

Available features follow your account and credits.

Customers can register and explore CallFocus. Some AI calls, voice notes and media features require purchased credits. New permanent voice-cloning slots cost 1,000 CallFocus credits and payment is deducted once when the purchase is confirmed. Existing reservations are not charged again. Availability and terms can change in the admin settings.

Frequently asked

Questions about CallFocus services

Does CallFocus offer voice cloning from a real recording?

Yes. CallFocus supports consent-based permanent voice cloning. Eligible signed-in adults can record or upload their own voice; the service requires an available slot and provider capacity.

Is permanent voice cloning the same as a custom AI voice profile?

No. A custom AI voice profile adjusts supported synthetic-voice characteristics. Permanent voice cloning creates a reusable saved voice based on a consenting adult user’s recording.

Can I use my saved voice for calls and voice notes?

A successfully created permanent voice can be reused for compatible cloned calls and voice notes, subject to service availability and credits.

Does CallFocus offer image-to-video generation?

Yes. The Image to Video tool takes an uploaded image and motion instructions and produces a short AI-generated video when enabled.

Are all services free?

Account creation and browsing are separate from usage. Calls, voice notes, video generation and other eligible features can use CallFocus credits. Purchasing a new permanent voice-cloning slot costs 1,000 CallFocus credits, deducted once after purchase confirmation; previous reservations are not charged retroactively.
